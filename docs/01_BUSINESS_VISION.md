# **KIZUNAFIT BUSINESS VISION**

---

# **Introduction**

KIZUNAFIT is a Fitness Coaching Marketplace and Coaching Management Platform designed to connect clients with independent fitness trainers and manage the entire coaching lifecycle through a unified ecosystem.

The platform combines trainer discovery, consultation management, payments, coaching delivery, progress tracking, and communication into a single structured platform.

KIZUNAFIT exists because modern online fitness coaching is fragmented, difficult to manage, difficult to scale, and highly dependent on disconnected tools.

The platform aims to replace fragmented coaching workflows with a professional coaching ecosystem built around structured coaching relationships.

# **Industry Background**

The online fitness coaching industry continues to grow rapidly.

# **Business Model**

KIZUNAFIT operates as a managed marketplace.

Revenue is generated through platform fees added to coaching purchases.

Example:

Trainer Fee      ₹12,000

Platform Fee     ₹1,500

Client Pays      ₹13,500

The trainer receives the coaching fee.

KIZUNAFIT retains the platform fee.

# **Version 1 Constraints**

## **Platform**

## **Geographic Scope**

---

## **Trainer Registration**

---

## **Marketplace Model**

One Active Coaching Relationship Per Client

A client may browse, evaluate, and compare multiple trainers before purchasing coaching.

However, a client may have only one active coaching relationship at a time in Version 1\.

---

## **Acquisition Model**

One Active Acquisition Pipeline Per Client

A client may only participate in one active trainer acquisition journey at a time.

This simplifies marketplace operations and prevents overlapping acquisition flows.

---
